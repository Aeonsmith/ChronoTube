# 🪐 Reconstructing Lost Internet History: Introducing the ChronoTube Temporal Graph Engine

## 🏛️ The Challenge: The Illusion of Complete Web Archives
When archiving platforms crawl dynamic platforms like YouTube:
* Large video files are rarely archived due to bandwidth constraints.
* JavaScript-heavy Single Page Applications (SPAs) render as blank boxes in historical replays.
* Multi-month indexing blackouts (such as the widespread capture gaps in 2023) leave vast historical blind spots.
* Platform algorithms (5-star ratings to watch-time to neural vectors) are absent from static HTML captures.

## 💡 What is the ChronoTube Engine?
The **ChronoTube Engine** is an open-source temporal simulation operating system and graph engine designed for digital archaeology and internet preservation across 2005–2026+.

## 🧮 Mathematical Gap Resolution & Slerp Vectors
* **`LOGARITHMIC_GROWTH`**: Models natural sub-linear decay of view count accumulation ($v(t) = \exp(\ln v_0 + t(\ln v_1 - \ln v_0))$).
* **`SIGMOID_S_CURVE`**: Models algorithmic inflection and saturation plateaus for trending content.
* **`POLYNOMIAL_ACCELERATION`**: Captures quadratic takeoff ($t^2$) during rapid breakout moments.
* **`DISCRETE_BOUNDED`**: Enforces strict historical constraints (e.g. legacy star ratings bounded between 1.00 and 5.00).
* **`SPHERICAL_SLERP`**: Spherical Linear Interpolation for 1536-dim semantic embeddings with unit-norm preservation.
